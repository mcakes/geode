# Review: geode-timeseries, geode-chart, geode-widgets

## Summary

The three crates are unusually disciplined: `geode-chart` is genuinely pure
presentation, the `ChartKey`/chrome-key memoisation is real and pinned by rebuild
counters rather than by hope, and every pointer gesture does route through the same
action id its key takes.
Correctness findings cluster in three places: the displayed-time offset is a scalar
snapshot of "now" so historical spans are labelled at the wrong offset; the stats
window is derived from the *previous* delivery's buckets, so a frequency change while
zoomed queries the wrong span; and `ChartKey` is sound only by a chain of four
arguments, one of which is factually wrong about `percentiles`.
Performance sits inside the documented budgets and the known path-clone gap is
honestly recorded; the one uncached per-frame cost left is the percentile *tag*
`Vec<Text>`, rebuilt with a `SharedString` clone per tag every frame.
Keyboard parity is complete for every pointer gesture except the crosshair readout,
which is hover-only — a philosophy §2 gap rather than a bug.
`geode-widgets` holds exactly one widget, is correctly host-agnostic, and its one
latent coupling (`Segment::at` by painted index) is documented but unenforced.

## Findings

### Critical

None. No unwrap on user-reachable data, no panic path in the geometry, no
allocation-in-paint that breaks a stated budget, and no lens-not-brain violation: the
only arithmetic in `src/` is coordinate mapping, and expression lowering is
add/sub/mul/div with a guarded division
(`crates/geode-data/src/query/series.rs:88`).

### Major

**M1. The displayed-time offset is a scalar snapshot of the zone's offset *now*, so a
chart of historical data labels every bucket at today's DST phase.**
`crates/geode-timeseries/src/tile/mod.rs:1248-1254` produces `offset_secs` as
`clock.local(Utc::now()).offset().fix().local_minus_utc()` — the offset in force at the
moment of the rebuild. It is baked into `ChartModel.offset_secs`
(`crates/geode-timeseries/src/core/chart.rs:46-56`) and is the only zone information
`geode-chart` ever sees: `ticks` resolves it into one `FixedOffset` for the whole call
(`crates/geode-chart/src/core/time.rs:245-249`), `Unit::label`/`value`/`floor`/`next`
all run in that fixed offset (`:130-196`), and `bucket_title` uses it for the readout
(`crates/geode-chart/src/element.rs:559-568`).
Consequences: under `Continuous`, day/month/year candidates are generated by
`unit.ceil` then repeated `unit.next` in that fixed offset
(`time.rs:393-412`), so "midnight" gridlines across a DST transition are an hour off
the real local midnight and the `%H:%M` labels state a time the bucket does not start
at. Under `Session` the labels shift uniformly (less visible), but the *tick placement*
can also move: candidates are the buckets where `unit.value` changes against the
previous bucket (`time.rs:373-390`), so a bucket near a day boundary can be classified
into the wrong day and the month/day tick lands one bucket early or late.
*Impact*: silently wrong axis labels and gridline positions on any chart whose span
crosses a DST change — which for the default `1y` range is most charts, for half the
year. The data is correct; the axis lies about it.
*Direction*: carry the zone rather than the offset. `geode-chart` already depends on
`geode-core`, and `geode_core::clock::Clock` exposes `zone()`/`local(t)`, so the chart
can resolve the offset *per displayed instant* without gaining a shell dependency.
Failing that, document in the chart README that continuous ticks are fixed-offset and
name the failure.

**M2. The stats window is computed from the previous delivery's buckets, so a
frequency change while zoomed asks percentiles and density over the wrong span.**
`crates/geode-timeseries/src/core/request.rs:14-34` maps a `Session` view — an *index*
window — onto timestamps by indexing `buckets`, and
`crates/geode-timeseries/src/tile/data.rs:181-190` passes the buckets of the *last
answer*. `Model::set_frequency` returns `SETTING` (= QUERY|CHROME|SESSION)
(`crates/geode-timeseries/src/core/model.rs:535-548`), so `apply_changed` requeries
immediately and nothing re-bases the view's indices first: `reset_view` is armed only
on a FETCH (`crates/geode-timeseries/src/tile/mod.rs:760-770`), and a frequency change
carries none.
So `window` returns `(buckets[lo], buckets[hi-1] + new_step)` where `lo`/`hi` are
indices into the *old* bucketing at the *new* step. It is clamped inside `range`
(`request.rs:52-56`), so the span stays legal — it is simply not the visible one.
*Impact*: ruling 10 says stats are over the visible window. With percentiles or
density on, a `f`/`F` step or a `:freq` while zoomed produces percentile lines and
density bins computed over a different span, shown with no marker. It self-corrects on
the next delivery only because `set_full` then re-bases.
*Direction*: record the frequency the held result was built under (beside
`result_seq`) and have `window` fall back to the whole range when it differs — or reset
the view on a frequency change, which is what a changed bucketing arguably means
anyway.

**M3. `ChartKey` excludes `percentiles` on a rationale that is factually wrong, and the
key is sound only by a second argument that a refused requery breaks.**
The doc at `crates/geode-timeseries/src/tile/mod.rs:92-97` states `percentiles`
"never reaches the chart model — they shape the REQUEST, and the answer arrives as a
new `result`". But `crates/geode-timeseries/src/core/chart.rs:29-45` copies
`r.percentiles` into every `ChartSlot` *and* derives `percentile_labels` from them, and
`crates/geode-chart/src/element.rs:436-499` draws both the lines and the tags from
those fields. The key is nonetheless *sound* in the happy path, because
`result_seq` is in it and any percentile change that alters the drawn levels arrives as
a new result.
That second argument fails on a refused requery. `toggle_percentiles`
(`model.rs:682-691`) returns `SETTING`; `apply_changed` calls `requery`; if
`data.series(params)` returns false, `requery` sets a notice and returns without
touching `result` or `result_seq`
(`crates/geode-timeseries/src/tile/data.rs:196-215`). `rebuild_chrome` then computes a
`chart_key` identical to the stored one and takes the early return
(`tile/mod.rs:895-910`), so the chart keeps painting the percentile lines and tags the
trader just switched off.
*Impact*: after "series request refused: the data service is busy or gone", the chart
shows statistics that are no longer requested, with the notice naming the refusal but
nothing marking the lines as stale. Violates the invariant the crate wrote down for
itself ("A field `chart::build` reads must appear here") in letter, and philosophy §3
in effect.
*Direction*: add the percentile fractions (or their count plus bit patterns) to
`ChartKey` — the rule the crate stated. Alternatively make a refused requery
invalidate the chart model; the key fix is narrower and matches the documented
contract.

**M4. Percentile tags are rebuilt, re-laid-out and re-collected every frame, outside
every cache.**
`crates/geode-chart/src/element.rs:472-499`.
The polylines and the dashed percentile *lines* sit behind `PathCaches`; the chrome —
side scales, y ticks, tick labels, x ticks — sits behind `chrome_key`
(`:238-332`, `:580-607`). The tags sit behind neither: per pane per frame the closure
walks every visible slot, zips `percentiles` with `percentile_labels`, maps each level
through `scale_y.y()` and `inside()`, allocates a `Text` with a cloned `SharedString`
and a `point`, `collect()`s into a fresh `Vec`, and hands it to
`PlotLabel::new(tags).paint(...)` — which lays the text out again.
The element's own module doc (`:36-46`) claims the whole per-frame allocation budget is
"the chrome `Vec`s the component's own painters take" plus the pinned path clone, and
`docs/current/performance.md`'s cache contracts say chart "paths and chrome are
cached". The tags are neither, and they are a function of exactly the chrome key's
inputs.
*Impact*: contradicts the documented allocation contract; bounded
(`MAX_PERCENTILES` = 8 × visible slots) so it does not threaten the 8 ms budget, but it
is per-frame heap churn on the render thread, which CLAUDE.md calls a reviewable
defect.
*Direction*: derive the tags in `derive_chrome` and store them in `Buffers` beside
`sides`; paint from the stored slice. The existing `chrome_rebuilds` counter then
covers them, and the "unchanged frame rebuilds nothing" test gains a real assertion
about text.

**M5. The crosshair readout has no keyboard route.**
It lives entirely in `Plot::tooltip_state`/`Plot::tooltip`
(`crates/geode-chart/src/element.rs:674-723`), both driven by a pointer position. There
is no x-cursor in the model (`crates/geode-timeseries/src/core/model.rs:102-119` holds a
*slot* cursor only), no action for it in the registered vocabulary
(`crates/geode-timeseries/src/content.rs:28-66`), and no binding in the keymap fragment
(`:76-140`).
PHILOSOPHY §2 is "every action reachable by mouse must be reachable by keyboard", and
the crate README advertises "cursor inspection" while `docs/current/features.md` claims
"Every verb has a pointer route beside its key" — the converse direction only. Reading a
bucket's values is the one capability the mouse has and the keyboard does not.
*Impact*: a trader on home row cannot read a value at a point on the chart. Philosophy
violation rather than a defect.
*Direction*: add an x-cursor to the model with its own keys, pass it to `ChartElement`
beside `view`. The chart already owns `Crosshair::at` and `ChartKey` already excludes
cursor movement by design, so the plumbing is one element parameter and costs no cache
invalidation — the architecture was clearly built expecting this.

### Minor

**m1. Density bars clamp out-of-domain bins to the strip edge — the exact behaviour the
percentile lines were changed to avoid.**
`crates/geode-chart/src/element.rs:523-537`. `top` and `bottom` are each
`.clamp(strip.y, strip.bottom())` and the height is `(bottom - top).max(1.0)`, so a bin
whose `(lo, hi)` lies entirely above or below the pane's visible domain collapses to a
1-px quad at the strip edge, painted at full width for its count. Fifty lines earlier
the crate argues the opposite case explicitly for percentiles: a level outside the pane
is *skipped*, because clamping "would park it on the pane's edge and read as a real
level at that value" (`:428-441`).
*Impact*: after a zoom into a quiet stretch, the density strip grows slivers at its top
and bottom that read as real mass at the extremes.
*Direction*: skip a bin whose span does not intersect `[scale.lo, scale.hi]`. Same rule
as the percentiles, and it also buys back quads against `MAX_DENSITY_QUADS`.

**m2. Tick rule 2 prefers the coarsest unit while rule 1 prefers the finest, and it is
the least-pinned of the four rules.**
`crates/geode-chart/src/core/time.rs:271-289`. Rule 1 is finest-first among units that
already fit; rule 2 is `(0..len).rev().find_map(...)`, coarsest-first among units that
keep `MIN_TICKS` after thinning. I could not construct a case where coarsest-first
gives a worse answer — for a coarse unit to pass rule 2 while failing rule 1 its
candidates must be crammed tighter than `tick_gap_px`, which means many of them, and
thinning them is then the right answer (20 years at 1200 px → decade ticks beats
months). So this is an observation, not a demonstrated defect. It is recorded because
the two directions are opposite, the comment does not say why, and the one test at the
boundary (`ticks_respect_the_gap`, `:606-628`) passes only because `Year` has a single
candidate at that shape — so the rule is asserted where it cannot fail.
*Direction*: add a test at a shape where rules 1 and 2 would choose different units
(two years at 300 px) stating which is intended, or note in the comment why coarsest is
right for rule 2 and finest for rule 1.

**m3. `View::clamp` silently discards a zoom when `full` shrinks.**
`crates/geode-chart/src/core/view.rs:64-79`, reached via `Model::set_full`
(`crates/geode-timeseries/src/core/model.rs:704-712`). `w` is
`span.max(MIN_SPAN.min(full_w)).min(full_w)`, so if a delivery returns fewer buckets
than before — a narrowed range, a source that lost coverage — a view zoomed wider than
the new extent is silently widened to it. `set_full`'s `was_full` check handles
widening well; the shrink case falls through to `pan(0.0, full)`, which clamps.
*Direction*: behaviour is defensible; add a line to `set_full`'s doc saying a shrinking
extent discards the zoom, so the next reader does not assume the pan-only branch is
lossless.

**m4. `axis_domain` pads a flat series by ±1.0 in data units, so a flat small-magnitude
series gets an axis labelled in whole numbers.**
`crates/geode-chart/src/core/scale.rs:150-163`: `let pad = if hi > lo { (hi - lo) *
DOMAIN_PAD } else { 1.0 }`. For a series flat at 0.0001 the y axis spans −1 to 1 and
`LinearScale::new` leaves the 2.0-wide domain alone (`:20-40`). Correct, never wrong,
unhelpful.
*Direction*: make the flat pad relative — `hi.abs().max(1.0) * DOMAIN_PAD` — so the
degenerate case scales with the value.

**m5. The density refusal message names the chart's internal quad constant to the
trader.**
`crates/geode-timeseries/src/core/model.rs:619-628`: "`{visible}` series × `{bins}`
bins would exceed the 2,000-bar bound". `MAX_DENSITY_QUADS` is a render-thread
implementation detail; the trader needs the effect and the remedy, both of which the
message already half-gives.
*Direction*: reword to the effect ("too many bars to draw — lower the bins or hide
series"). The guard itself is correct and correctly conservative.

**m6. `tooltip_state` and `tooltip` each re-solve the whole layout on every pointer
move, and `tooltip` re-`format!`s the title.**
`crates/geode-chart/src/element.rs:674-723`. Both call `self.layout(bounds)`, which runs
`Layout::solve` including `layout_options` — and `layout_options` walks every slot four
times through `uses()` (`crates/geode-chart/src/model.rs:88-104`). `tooltip` then calls
`bucket_title` (`:559-568`), which builds a `DateTime` and `format!`s `%Y-%m-%d %H:%M`
per frame while the pointer rests. `paint` solves the layout a third time.
*Impact*: three layout solves and a formatted string per hovering frame. Small — slots
are few — but it is the one geometry path with no memo, in a crate whose whole design
is memoised geometry.
*Direction*: cache the solved layout in `Buffers` under the chrome key (it is a pure
function of the same inputs) and read it from all three phases; prepare the title
alongside.

**m7. `y_tick_hint`'s guard is correct but fragile.**
`crates/geode-chart/src/element.rs:272-277`:
`(h / design_px(Y_TICK_GAP, self.rem_px)).max(2.0) as usize`. The `.max(2.0)` is on the
float, before the cast, so the hint is never 0 and `ticks`' zero-hint path
(`scale.rs:70-95`, tested at `:196-202`) is unreachable from here. Verified safe.
Recorded only because moving `.max` after the cast would silently produce an axis with
no ticks on a short pane.

**m8. `RANGE_HINT` advertises the preset digits unconditionally, contradicting the rule
in exactly the state where a trader reads it.**
`crates/geode-timeseries/src/popup.rs:76-78` paints the static
"1–7 preset · tab switches · enter commits", while
`RangePopup::digit_is_preset` (`:301-320`) makes a digit a preset only while
`!edited && !typing()` — and its own doc states the learnable rule as "a digit is a
preset *until you start editing a date*, and the date's from then on".
*Impact*: after typing into a field the hint still offers presets that the next digit
will not trigger.
*Direction*: make the hint follow `edited`. The popup already re-prepares its segments
on every key, so the state is to hand and there is no per-frame cost.

**m9. `Axis::next`/`prev` `unwrap()` a position over their own exhaustive array.**
`crates/geode-chart/src/core/axis.rs:46-56`. Infallible — `ALL` holds all four variants
— but it is an `unwrap` where an exhaustive `match` would let the compiler guarantee
what the unwrap asserts. Same shape as `Frequency::index`'s
`.expect("every frequency is in ALL")` (`crates/geode-core/src/series/mod.rs:84-89`).
*Direction*: replace both with matches.

**m10. `separator_before`/`Segment::at` assume a prefix precision, enforced only by a
comment.**
`crates/geode-widgets/src/datefield/paint.rs:29-46` and the click callback at
`:80-86`. The doc states the coupling precisely ("`i` is the PAINTED index, and it
equals `Segment::index()` only because every `Precision` shows a PREFIX of the six
segments") and even names the fix (`SegmentText` carrying its own `Segment`). Nothing
fails if a non-prefix precision is added — clicks would select the wrong segment,
silently.
*Direction*: put `segment: Segment` on `SegmentText`; it is two lines in `segments()`
(`crates/geode-widgets/src/datefield/mod.rs:402-429`) and retires the invariant.

**m11. `thousands` is a third copy of digit grouping in the workspace.**
`crates/geode-timeseries/src/core/model.rs:762-780`, whose own comment records that
`geode_core::series::group_thousands` and `geode_core::format::group_thousands` are both
private and "neither is worth exporting for one message". Three implementations,
documented as deliberate.
*Direction*: export one from `geode-core` and delete two.

**m12. `geode-chart` dev-depends on `geode-shell` for a single assertion.**
`crates/geode-chart/Cargo.toml` dev-dependencies, declared and justified (the
bundled-theme palette sweep, mirroring `geode-marketdata`). Dev-deps do not reach the
shipped graph, so the layering claim in the crate doc holds. Recorded because it makes
`cargo test -p geode-chart` build the shell — the slowest thing in the workspace — for
one test.
*Direction*: none required; if test time matters, move the sweep to a shell-side test
that calls `Palette::from_theme`.

**m13. Two capture-phase mouse-down handlers in the header, neither stopping
propagation — verified correct, recorded as a trap.**
`crates/geode-timeseries/src/header.rs:262-268` (the `range · freq` readout) and
`:427-433` (the `⋯` button). Both use `capture_any_mouse_down`, both guard on
`event.button != MouseButton::Left`, neither stops propagation. Each sits on its own
element so a single click reaches only one, and both handlers are toggles, so the
capture-phase choice (documented at both sites, ahead of an open popup's
`on_mouse_down_out`) is right and the missing `stop_propagation` is required for the
shell's click-to-focus. No defect. Recorded because the combination — capture phase,
no propagation stop, toggle semantics — is three easily-broken invariants in eight
lines, and the reasoning lives only in the comments.

### Idea

**I1. TODO "range dialog correlating number with preset hard, brainstorm better" —
what is done, and the cheap fix.**
Already done: the seven presets are labelled chips (`1w`…`5y`), each clickable and each
committing at once (`crates/geode-timeseries/src/popup.rs:917-953`); the digits `1`–`7`
map positionally through `Preset::digit`
(`crates/geode-timeseries/src/core/range.rs:60-63`); the hint names the digit range.
The difficulty the TODO names is real and structural: the chip reads `3m` and its key is
`3`, but the chip reads `1y` and its key is `5`. Nothing on screen connects them, so the
mapping has to be counted.
*Proposal*: paint the index on the chip. The action menu already solves exactly this
problem by putting the live chord in a trailing column
(`crates/geode-timeseries/src/popup.rs:1100-1140` builds `trailing` from
`chord_for`), so the pattern and the vocabulary exist — a leading or trailing digit on
each preset chip needs no new state and no new mechanism. The alternative, keying
presets by their own initial (`w`, `m`, `y`…), reads better but collides (`1m`/`3m`/`6m`
all start `m`) and would break shipped bindings, which philosophy §2 calls a promise.
The label is the cheaper and safer answer.

**I2. TODO "better mouse interaction" is essentially complete; the one gesture missing
is bounds-by-drag.**
Verified done: swatch click toggles visibility through `v`'s own path
(`crates/geode-timeseries/src/tile/popups.rs:869-877`); the wheel zooms about the
pointer with the dominant axis winning
(`crates/geode-timeseries/src/tile/pointer.rs:100-140`); a plot drag pans (`:187-210`);
a divider drag moves the split (`:211-240`); the readout opens the range popup
(`popups.rs:803-812`), which now also carries a frequency row whose chips write at once
(`popup.rs:985-1046`).
*Not done*: rubber-band zoom — dragging a horizontal span to zoom to it, the first
gesture anyone arriving from another charting tool tries. Every piece is present:
`Hit::Plot` already returns a `fraction`, `Drag` is a two-variant enum with room for a
third, and `View::zoom`+`pan` compose into "set to this span".
*Proposal*: add `Drag::Band { from_fraction }`, armed by a `shift`-modified press —
`chart_pressed` currently refuses *any* modified press
(`pointer.rs:157-160`) and the shell owns the mod-key press, so `shift` is free. Paint
a translucent band while dragging; on release map the two fractions into view units and
assign. End it at `view_moved` like the other two gestures, per the README's rule.
Keyboard parity already exists through `=`/`-`/`0`, so this is pointer convenience, not
a new capability — which is the right side of philosophy §2 to be on.

**I3. Mark the chart stale while a query for a changed range or frequency is in
flight.**
Follows from M2 and M3. `apply_changed` requeries and re-prepares the header, but the
header's `range · freq` readout is built from the *model*
(`crates/geode-timeseries/src/header.rs:98-131` via `Model::header_text`) while the
pixels still show the old delivery. So between request and answer the strip reads
`3m · 1h` over a chart that is still `1y · 1d`. Philosophy §3 accepts known-stale
clearly marked and rejects ambiguity; this is currently ambiguous.
*Proposal*: the tile already tracks `query_in_flight`
(`crates/geode-timeseries/src/tile/mod.rs:157`). Tint the readout, or dim the chart,
while a query is outstanding whose frequency or range differs from the held result's.
One bool into `render`, no new state — and it is the same recorded-frequency field M2
needs, so the two fixes share their cost.

## Systemic patterns

1. **"The key must contain everything the build reads" is stated twice and honoured
   once and a half.** Both `ChartKey`'s doc
   (`crates/geode-timeseries/src/tile/mod.rs:76-99`) and the chrome key's
   (`crates/geode-chart/src/element.rs:14-34`) state the invariant explicitly and name
   the failure it prevents. The chrome key is complete. `ChartKey` is sound but not
   complete (M3), and its exclusion list has grown one bespoke rationale per entry —
   one of which is wrong about the code. A key whose correctness rests on four
   independent arguments rather than on a rule is one refactor from breaking, and the
   mutation harness cannot tell "sound" from "complete".

2. **A zone is carried as a scalar offset.** `offset_secs: i32` flows from `AppClock`
   through `ChartKey` and `ChartModel` into `Unit::label` and `bucket_title`. It is
   right for one instant and wrong for a span (M1). The same scalar appears in four
   types, so widening it later touches all four — and the module doc's claim that the
   offset makes displayed times "the trader's local clock" is true only for today.

3. **Clamp-vs-skip is decided per drawable, not by a rule.** Percentile lines skip when
   outside the pane, with an explicit argument for why clamping would lie
   (`element.rs:428-441`). Percentile tags skip (they reuse `inside`). Density bars
   clamp (m1). One question, three answers, one of them the answer the crate argued
   against in writing.

4. **Pointer/keyboard parity is enforced by construction in one direction only.** Every
   pointer door dispatches an action id; the README pins it and the tests exercise it
   through real clicks. Nothing checks the converse, and the crosshair is where it is
   missing (M5). The architecture anticipated the fix — `ChartKey` excludes cursor
   movement on purpose — which makes the omission look like unfinished work rather than
   a decision.

5. **Provenance citations are dense enough to be load-bearing.** `popup.rs` carries 17
   spec/round/date references, `tile/mod.rs` and `header.rs` 14 each. CLAUDE.md asks
   that a comment not require a task number to make sense; most of these do also state
   the invariant, so they pass — but the density means a reader now navigates by
   citation, and a stale citation would mislead rather than merely fail to help.

## What is done well

- **`geode-chart` really is pure presentation.** No `geode-shell` in the shipped deps,
  no series/source/tile vocabulary anywhere under `core/`, the rem arrives as a
  parameter, and `DESIGN_REM` is duplicated with a comment saying exactly why it must
  be (`crates/geode-chart/src/core/mod.rs:22-26`). The single `geode-core` dependency is
  for the colour floor alone. The example binary carries the percentile and bin
  arithmetic and states in its own doc that this is precisely why it is not in `src/`
  (`crates/geode-chart/examples/chart.rs:12-18`) — the lens rule enforced at a crate
  boundary rather than asserted in a README.
- **The rebuild counters are the right instrumentation.** `rebuilds`,
  `chrome_rebuilds` and `density_quads` as thread-locals, with the per-thread choice
  justified against parallel window tests, let the tests assert "an unchanged frame
  rebuilt nothing" as a number (`crates/geode-chart/src/element.rs:1050-1096`). The
  density test measures across one deliberate frame and explains why that count, unlike
  the others, is not idempotent (`:1186-1213`).
- **Min-max decimation is correct and property-tested.** Every column keeps its min and
  max in x order, a NaN breaks the polyline with no leading, trailing or doubled break,
  and the output buffer is reused — the first three pinned by a proptest over 400 points
  × 40 columns, the fourth by a pointer-identity assertion
  (`crates/geode-chart/src/core/decimate.rs:106-183`). The module doc names the failure
  it exists to prevent ("lyon's collapse is not decimation"), which is the difference
  between a comment and documentation.
- **Session vs continuous is a real distinction, tested at its boundary.** A weekend gap
  has no width under `Session` and three days' width under `Continuous`, asserted
  directly (`time.rs:441-492`). `full()`, `visible()`, `centre()` and the crosshair are
  each mode-aware and each tested including empty, single-bucket and
  zoomed-past-the-gap cases.
- **The fetch/query/barrier state machine is unusually careful.** `in_flight` plus
  `in_flight_range` as the "already asked" half kept distinct from
  `SlotState::Fetching`; `prune_in_flight` at every departure; `Ok(0)` still triggering
  a query because coverage is *known*; a refused submit still answering the barrier and
  *then* clearing `acted`, in that order, with the reason spelled out
  (`crates/geode-timeseries/src/tile/data.rs:196-215`). Nearly every one of these is a
  bug someone found and closed with a comment naming the symptom rather than the fix.
- **One closer, one door, one tail.** `close_popup_with_window` blurs conditionally on
  the popup's *own* field holding focus, so a field orphaned by a tile-focus move cannot
  blur the command line; `add_pair` is the single add door for `:add` and the picker;
