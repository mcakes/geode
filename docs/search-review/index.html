How much of the tree should search keep?

The same blotter, query, columns, and selection. Three ways to keep your bearings.

Focus a table: ↑ ↓ select · Enter reveal · Esc restore

Keep the gutter’s existing meaning. On / Relative / Off follow the app setting. Numbers address the rows currently displayed, including ancestor rows. Relative numbers follow the active row, just as they do in the normal table.

Chevrons should do something. In B and C they fold branches only within the search view. Enter reveals the selected row in the original tree; Escape restores the original expansion. Group totals remain the original totals, not sums of matching rows.

Try “spx”, “5000”, “ndx”, or an empty query. Click a chevron to test folding.
